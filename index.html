Core System Architecture

A Decision-Support System for Safety Officers

SkyShield assists certified safety teams in organizing flight data, correlating past patterns, and highlighting potential risks. The platform provides advisory signals to augment human judgment.

Standardized Occurrence Capture

Structured reporting aligned with ICAO Annex 13 taxonomy, capturing flight phases, weather conditions, system telemetry, and pilot narratives.

Pattern Match & Similarity Search

Identifies historical incident clusters across aircraft types and flight phases, surfacing recurring factors without claiming to predict crashes.

Root-Cause Decision Support

Evaluates potential contributing factors across maintenance, human factors, weather, and operational domains to support rigorous investigation.
